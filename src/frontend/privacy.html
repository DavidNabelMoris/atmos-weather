Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Atmos Weather is a simple weather lookup app. This page explains what data it handles and what it doesn't.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts, sign-ups, or passwords.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising SDKs.
  • No cookies are set by this app.
  • We never sell or share your data — we don't collect enough of it to.

Data used to provide the forecast

  • When you search for or select a city, that text is sent from your browser to our server, which forwards it to WeatherAPI.com to fetch current conditions and the forecast. We don't log or store these searches.
  • The current weather condition (e.g. "rain at night") is used to request a matching background photo from Pixabay through our server. No personal information is included in that request.
  • Our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically records standard server logs (such as IP address and request timing) as part of normal infrastructure operation. We don't access these logs for anything beyond debugging outages.

Favorites & local storage

Cities you save to Favorites are stored only in your own browser's local storage, on your device. They are never sent to our server or to any third party. You can remove a favorite at any time from the Favorites menu, or clear it entirely by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Third-party services

Weather data is provided by WeatherAPI.com and background photography by Pixabay. Your use of those underlying services through this app is also subject to their own privacy policies: WeatherAPI.com Terms · Pixabay Terms.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change too. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the project's GitHub issues.
